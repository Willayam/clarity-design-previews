S8: auth routes, before and after

Before is the surfaces PR head (#715). After is S8: LoginRoute.css is deleted, and the login, forgot-password and reset-password pages run on the shared Button, Input, Badge and Label primitives. The submit button, SSO tiles, password eye toggle, "Create an account." link and "Last used" tag are Buttons and a Badge; the glass field look is Input variant="glass".

Parity came first: against a base captured twice, the control-state gallery showed zero of 121,905 values changed and all 77 screenshots were identical, across light and dark at 1440, 1025, 1024, 1023, 768 and 390px. Error states come from stubbed /auth responses; nothing reached the real sign-in service. The differences below are the normalize wave, including three fixes to the old page: wrong-password errors now draw a red field border (the old stylesheet hid it), the forgot and reset password pages centre their panel at 1024px and wider, and the password field's open and close animation now works outside Chromium. Notes snap from 13.1px to 14px, so the sign-in error message wraps to two lines. A locked-out account does not mark its disabled password field invalid.
