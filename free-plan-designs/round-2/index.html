1. Banner treatments the top bar, six ways

Same place as round 1, a strip across the top of the content, and the same four states with the same words. What changes is how it looks: fill or none, where the colour sits, what counts the days, and whether the action is a button or a link. Pick a treatment; all four states follow, on desktop and phone.

2. Trial-ended moment five concepts, none of them the round-1 dialog

Why round 1 read as ugly, so none of it comes back:

  • The half-black scrim turned the whole screen grey, so a calm moment looked like an error.
  • Six bullet points in two columns made a checklist, with nothing for the eye to land on.
  • Five levels of type inside a 512px box, with an icon in a circle on every line.
  • Nothing showed the plan; everything was told.

Every concept keeps the copy rule: what stays comes first, with the rep's own counts, then what changes.

3. Made with Clarity badge six variants on the prospect's page

Each variant sits on the same Journey Page, drawn with the real public stylesheet. The frames scroll. In every variant the badge is smaller, greyer and further from the eye than the rep's own call to action, and nothing ever touches the video.
