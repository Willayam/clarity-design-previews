Clarity simplicity audit

14 October 2026 · Six code audits (shell and library, editor, blocks and prospect page, recorder, share and stats, settings) plus a read-only pull of production usage. Nothing was changed.

The test for every control was whether it helps a client record, build a page, send it, or act on what the prospect did. Most of the product passes. The surface that fails falls into three groups. Some choices almost nobody makes, like themes, fonts, corners, four layouts and four button types. Some actions are reachable three or four ways. And one thing often has three or four names.

Three problems matter more than any button. They break the promise itself.

  1. The page is not built around the video. Clarity transcribes every recording, but the transcript only feeds auto-titles and auto-trim. Page generation never reads it (workers/frontend-router/src/journey-page-generation.ts). A rep who records and clicks "Open page" gets a blank composer and has to type the context they just said out loud.
  2. The rep can't see a prospect who clicks "buy". Pricing buttons are rendered without a tracking id (render-values.ts:77), so the click is never recorded. Every one of the 1,138 tracked clicks in production came from a hero button.
  3. The "call now" signal is off by default. Of the four view-email toggles, only "watched 60%" is on. "Started watching" is off, and clicking a button sends no email at all. Stats rows also say "Today" with no hour.

The single-block number changes the priorities. The fastest win is making the one-block page great. That means a strong default look, one clear button, and a link copied right after recording. Most of the editor can then sit behind "Add section" without slowing anyone down.

The ten changes that matter most

  1. Feed the video's transcript into page generation, and open the composer automatically. After recording, "Open page" should become "Build page". The editor opens with the composer showing and the transcript attached, and the rep answers one question: who is this for? This is the "under two minutes" promise. Fix
  2. Track every button on the prospect page. Give pricing and document buttons their own ids so "clicked Choose this plan" reaches the rep. Fix
  3. One notification switch, on by default: "Email me when a prospect watches", fired on start, with how far they got in the body. Add "clicked a button" as a trigger. It replaces four toggles. Merge
  4. Cut the style panel to presets plus "More". Delete Size and Corners because the preset sets them. Put Fonts, Background and the custom color picker behind "More". Brand color comes from Settings. Logo lives on the canvas only. 93% of customer pages never set a theme. DeleteHide
  5. Two hero buttons, no button types. Delete the "Primary" switch and the Sign / Payment / Custom slots. The first button is the main one. Default labels become "Book a call" and "Learn more"; today "Custom" and "Calendar" can show up on a prospect's page. DeleteRewrite
  6. One Settings page instead of five pages plus Profile. Brand, Notifications, Plan, Your account. Delete Address, Timezone, Work and Home phone (nothing reads them), the read-only Email field and the duplicate Company field. Replace the Password page with a reset-email button. MergeDelete
  7. One stats view with one vocabulary. Video stats is a separate 583-line modal that sorts differently from page stats. Merge it into the page stats table, newest first, with "12m ago" instead of "Today". Use "visits" for pages and "plays" for video, everywhere. Delete the clickable mini-page preview, the "3 of 5 watching" scrub readout and the retention curve under five plays. MergeDelete
  8. Make Record the first action on Home. Today the button order is Upload, Record video, New Journey Page, and the filled button is New Journey Page. Record goes first and filled. The empty state "Your workspace is empty" becomes "Record your first video". Rewrite
  9. Take Learn and Contacts out of the main nav. Learn is nine static videos in four tabs, and some predate the current editor; move it to "Help" in the account menu. Contacts is a mini CRM (Department, Industry, Import CSV) that never shows which page a lead came from. Replace it with per-page "Leads" showing source page and date. HideMerge
  10. Replace the trim toggles with automatic trim. The finish screen shows seven toggles above three buttons. Auto-trim applies silently. The handles stay, and one "Restore full video" link appears only when a trim is set. "Preview" becomes "Thumbnail". Four recording layouts become three: Camera, Screen, Camera + screen. DeleteMerge

Decisions that are yours

  • Billing UI. Should the Stripe portal replace the in-app card list, and should the cancel survey go?
  • Pricing and summary. Pricing is on 7% of customer pages and summary on 3%. Fold them into one "Pricing and scope" block, or keep both? I'd keep pricing, fold summary into it, and drop the three-tier chip.
  • Contacts. Replace it with per-page Leads, or keep the CRM for the few who import CSVs?
  • Notifications. One switch fired on "started watching", or keep "started" and "finished" as two? The auditors split. I'd ship one switch with depth in the email.

  • Billing never loads the default card id, so the real default shows no badge and can be deleted like any other card. BillingRoute.tsx:92
  • Branding save failures are swallowed with no message. BrandingRoute.tsx:80
  • The trial wall sells monthly only; the Subscription page sells monthly and annual.
  • 401 stored form submissions against 65 page_form_submit events, so submit tracking undercounts.
  • A notification preference, "someone shared my video", defaults to on and has no UI.
  • Stale comments: AddSection.tsx says six tiles and no repeats (there are eight, and repeats are allowed). CtaRowEditor.tsx refers to a side panel that no longer exists. CONTEXT.md puts title, description and button on video Edit metadata, but they live on the hero block.
