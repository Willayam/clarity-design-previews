Finish your video: states and errors

Round two. Before is the finish screen on main, and after is this branch. Everything is rendered from a scripted offline take with mock data.

How errors work now.

  • Every error row has a title saying what failed, one line on what is still safe or what to check, and a button that fixes it.
  • Rows appear under the action buttons, so nothing you are about to click moves.
  • When a row's fix works, the row goes away.
  • Red means something failed. Amber means you can carry on another way.
