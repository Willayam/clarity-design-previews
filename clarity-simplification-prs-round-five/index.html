Notifications: one switch, on by default, fired when they start

PR #1510

Four toggles decided which watch milestones emailed the rep, and only 'watched 60%' was on by default. So a prospect starting to watch sent nothing, and a button click never sent anything. 93% of customers never touched the toggles and nobody has changed one in the new app (D6 brief). Now one switch, on by default, sends an email when a prospect starts watching, then at most one an hour with how far they got and which buttons they clicked. On 2026-10-19 William asked for much shorter copy, so the switch has no helper text and the emails keep only what a rep acts on.

What changes (9)
  • Settings > Notifications: four switches ('Someone starts watching', 'A viewer watches 60%', 'A viewer watches 80%', 'A viewer watches to the end') -> one switch 'Email me when someone watches', on by default, with no helper text. The card description above the old switches is gone too.
  • Round five copy cut on the switch: label 'Email me when someone watches a page' -> 'Email me when someone watches'; helper 'The first email arrives when they start watching. After that, at most one email an hour with how far they got and which buttons they clicked.' -> removed.
  • The first view email now goes when a prospect starts watching. Before, it went at 60%, because 'started' was off by default.
  • A button click on a published page now emails the owner: at once after a quiet hour ('Someone clicked "Book a call"'), otherwise inside the next hourly email ('Watched 100% of "Q4 demo" and clicked "Book a call"'). Viewers who clicked are listed first.
  • Single view email, cut: the intro 'A visitor on your page "Acme Q4 proposal" started watching "Q4 demo".' (it restated the title), 'So far they have watched 13% of the video.' and 'They watched from Oslo, Norway.' One line replaces all three: 'On "Acme Q4 proposal", from Oslo, Norway.' The title, the 'See page stats' button and the footer stay.
  • Hourly summary email, cut: the intro 'Here is who watched since your last view email.', the per-page count line ('2 people', '1 person'), and the page heading when the email covers one page (the title already names it). Multi-page summaries keep a heading per page.
  • Email footer: 'Choose which of these emails you get in Notification settings.' -> 'Turn these emails off in Notification settings.' Kept, since it is the only way out of these emails.
  • Agent tools settings_get/settings_update: notifications.viewStarted/watched60/watched80/watchedToEnd -> notifications.viewEmails
  • POST /api/notifications accepts only toggle_notification_when_someone_views_my_video; the shared, 60, 80 and 100 toggles answer 400
