What remains

Current-turn whole-token scrubbing deliberately replaces a company name such as Acme when it is also the password. It can also change that turn's pasted text. Values of at least six characters with both letters and another character are scrubbed thread-wide. Plain words or numeric values in earlier turns are scrubbed only in isolated replies of at most 64 characters. Same-step generator inputs scrub the value from all selected material, even earlier sources and even if the set is refused. Values can remain in approval input_json, other threads, unexecuted or refused sets, and earlier model calls. Existing page extras and earlier saved job bases can retain values; writer prompts omit extras. Initial model requests and live deltas can contain typed values. A hard crash after a set commits and before its scrub can still leave plaintext; a recovered password-bearing step will refuse generation rather than forward unprotected input. This does not claim complete plaintext removal.
