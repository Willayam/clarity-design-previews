Independent review fixes

The required review findings were confirmed against source. Reader declarations now use person and company fields. The host checks selected earlier messages as well as pasted sources, compares whole normalized companies after legal suffix removal, and otherwise compares whole person names. Colleagues at the same company pass under the owner’s explicit policy. General material has an explicit declaration. The host trusts those declarations; the trial counts another prospect’s material labelled general as a wrong-reader input.

The visible row uses the host’s source label and opening words, plus the opening words of selected earlier messages. App notes retain their chronological position; only notes after the current request move before it. Failed-turn notes identify their turn. The result cap measures escaped UTF-8 size and bounds retained guidance while retaining page ID, revision and status.

After a takeover, any password call in the turn blocks further generation and asks the rep to retry. An unfinished generation child also stops. Generating before setting the password remains controlled by the prompt. A password equal to the company is accepted and hides the company too. Those are disclosed limits.

Ask’s unpublish description now says approval. Never-published and already-off pages need no card. The card never promises to make a share link. MCP still unpublishes immediately. The draft-race fix also applies to MCP and session unpublish. A closer source read corrected the finding 8 assessment. The kernel does not compare the two bounds. One guard mode now lets unpublish ignore ordinary draft revisions while retaining owner, route, password and publication checks. A SQLite test edits the draft while the card waits and then approves the take-down. Findings 11, 12, 14 and 15 remain follow-ups as requested.

The inherited shared writer allows 0 to 8 stories instead of 1 to 8 and drops empty story, summary, FAQ and pricing blocks. These affect popover and MCP generation on merge. They belong in the shared generator scope, such as L1, rather than being described as Ask-only behavior. They were not moved.
