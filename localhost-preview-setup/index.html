Localhost previews on your Mac

Install once on each Mac. Start your app, then run localhost-preview 5173. Open the printed HTTPS link on your phone.

Install

The installer downloads Cloudflare's official macOS binary, checks its SHA-256 checksum, and installs it with the preview command in ~/.local/bin. It adds that directory to your zsh PATH. No administrator access or Cloudflare account is needed.

curl -fsSL https://willayam.github.io/clarity-design-previews/localhost-preview-setup/install.sh -o /tmp/install-localhost-preview.sh && bash /tmp/install-localhost-preview.sh

Read the installer

Use

Open a new terminal after installation. Keep your app running in its original terminal, then:

localhost-preview 5173

Replace 5173 with your app's port. The link works while your Mac is awake, online, and both processes are running. Press Ctrl-C to stop the tunnel. Each run produces a new public URL.

This exposes the selected local web server. App login, OAuth callbacks, and cookie settings may need their own preview configuration. Quick Tunnels do not support Server-Sent Events.
