Note for William
A healthy job can hit the cap. Media jobs have 32 attempts. auto_trim has a 30 s lease and 10 jobs per tick. mux_clip and mux_speech have 90 s leases, five jobs per tick and 20 per workspace. These limits do not guarantee completion within 32 claims. waitJob releases the lease without spending a failure, so successful polls consume attempts too. Speech waits 20 s between polls. Clip waits depend on asset age and range from 5 s to 600 s while Mux processes a clip. Therefore 32 times the lease is not a guaranteed minimum wait budget. This is the requested design note, not a failing assertion.
