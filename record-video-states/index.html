Record video, every state

36 screenshots of the recorder at staging.clarity.video, build 2619964c (main), taken 1 October 2026 at 1440 wide unless the title says 390. The camera and screen are Chrome's fake devices, which is why everything is green. Desktop shots are cropped to drop the app sidebar and empty space below.

Each card says when the state shows up, the exact words on screen, and how it was reached. "Real" means the app got there on its own. "Forced" means the script faked one browser input to get there. Click a screenshot for full size.

No screenshot

"Leave site?" prompt

Chrome's own dialog, so headless Chrome can't screenshot it. It shows if you reload or close the tab from Stop until the upload lands, including after a failed upload. It stops once the upload lands.

  • Leave site? Changes you made may not be saved. (Chrome writes this text, not Clarity.)

Checked in this run. The guard was on right after Stop and still on after the upload failed. It was off once the upload landed.

No camera or microphone

  • No camera or microphone found
  • Connect one, then reload.
  • Reload

Forced. The camera request was made to fail with NotFoundError, the error Chrome gives when no device exists.

Camera or microphone failed to start

Anything else, usually another app holding the camera.

  • Couldn't start your camera or microphone
  • Close other apps that might be using them, then reload.
  • Reload

Forced. The camera request was made to fail with NotReadableError, the error Chrome gives when another app holds the camera.
