Bare: contrast and play button variants

Bare with six ways to keep its controls readable on bright footage, and seven play buttons. None shows the length before play. The play buttons are shared by all three versions; switch the version in the prototype to see them on Hairline and Capsule. Throwaway prototype on Mux Player 3.13.4, Mux demo videos only.

Recommendation: Bare with Adaptive ink, and Adaptive glass for the play button. Both read the frame’s brightness twice a second from a 48×27 copy of the frame; the readout under the live player shows the measured value.

Contrast on bright footage

Paused, controls showing. The white title card at 0:03 is the worst case.

Soft shadow

Fails. On the white title card the controls almost vanish.

Halo

Fails. A heavier shadow only turns into a grey smudge on white.

Blur band

Works. A frosted band rises from the bottom edge. It costs a grey cast on white footage.

Adaptive scrim

Works. The dark fade only appears when the bottom of the frame is bright. On white footage it still reads as a grey band.

Adaptive ink

Recommended. The controls turn near-black on bright footage and stay white on dark, so nothing is drawn behind them at all. The paused button reads the center of the frame on its own.

Difference

Fails on color. Crisp on pure white, but over grass the controls turn a faint purple.

Play button

Before the first play.

White disc

The most legible everywhere. Reads as stock Apple TV.

Dark glass

Recedes beautifully on dark footage. Washes out to flat grey on bright footage.

Ring

Elegant on dark footage. Too faint on bright footage.

Glyph only

The quietest. Disappears on bright footage.

Brand glass

The brand color mixed into frosted glass, with a soft highlight and a brand-tinted shadow. Holds up on bright footage.

Adaptive glass

Recommended. Dark glass whose tint deepens with the brightness of the poster behind it: light on the talking head, near-charcoal on the bright sky.

Watch pill

Says what happens on click. The word adds weight that the other variants avoid.
