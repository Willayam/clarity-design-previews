Player chrome that recedes

Three implementations on the real Mux Player 3.13.4 that prospect pages ship, each as a custom theme. Every one is tested on a talking head (the sales case) and a bright video (the worst case for white controls). Rows: talking head, then bright. The prototype is throwaway; Mux’s demo videos, no customer data.

Open the live prototype

Refinement round, 5 Oct

  • Every center button now sits exactly on the frame center, measured at 0.0px on desktop and phone. The hidden loading spinner had been pushing them 22px low.
  • Play triangles are centered in their circles. They had drifted 8 to 22px left.
  • Center buttons and the Hairline pill scale with the player in three steps, so phones get smaller controls. On a phone the Hairline pill drops to disc plus length, 90px wide instead of 158.
  • Hairline’s pre-play is now a frosted pill with the brand color only in the play disc. White label text at readable contrast, even on the bright sky.
  • Paused is a single white glyph with a soft shadow in every version, instead of a tinted disc on the speaker’s face.
  • The frosted glass no longer picks up skin tone or turns olive. Saturation is down, tint is up.
  • The left and right edges of each control row line up. Speed text and icon strokes share one weight. Hairline’s progress line clears the rounded corners.
  • On touch, the scrubber thumb shows while paused.

What all three share

LessonHow it is implemented
Never dim the frameNo full-frame backdrop. Contrast comes only from what sits directly behind the controls.
Leave things outSix controls: play, mute (desktop), time, scrubber, speed, fullscreen. Captions appear only when the video has a track. No ±10s, no overflow menu, no volume slider, no PiP, AirPlay or Cast.
Disappear while watchingChrome and cursor hide after 2s of playback, never while paused. Fade in 0.18s, fade out 0.9s, with a slight downward drift.
Scrubber3px at rest, animates to 6px with a 12px thumb on hover, 24px hit area, storyboard preview with rounded corners.
First playOne big button with the length on it. The bar stays hidden until the first play. On the real page this lives in Clarity’s own click-to-play trigger, which already knows the duration.
One icon setCustom rounded icons: filled play and pause, 2px strokes everywhere else, no tooltips. Tabular figures for time.
Touch and phones44px targets on touch. Under 640px wide, mute and elapsed time drop out rather than squeezing the buttons. The paused play button lifts clear of the bar.
AccessibilityWhite 2px focus ring with a dark halo that survives bright video. Keyboard shortcuts kept (Space/K, J/L, M, F). Reduced motion turns off every transition.

1 · Hairline

Recommended. A frosted pre-play pill with the brand color in the play disc and the length beside it. While playing, the only thing on screen is a thin brand line along the bottom edge. On hover a soft, eased scrim rises behind the controls only. The full frame is never dimmed.

2 · Bare

The furthest recession. Nothing behind the controls: white glyphs and type with a layered soft shadow. Beautiful on the talking head. On the bright video the duration under the play button and the scrubber over sky stop being legible, which is the line it crosses.
