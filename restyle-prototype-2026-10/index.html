Restyle after recording

A prototype of the style panel for multi-track takes. The camera and the screen stay separate sources, so the layout, camera shape and background are a choice you make after Stop. Every frame here is drawn by the real packages/composition painter from PR #984, the same code the recorder now uses and the render container will use.

Sketch only: stock camera stills and a drawn dashboard stand in for a real take. Changes preview instantly. Apply simulates the real flow, where a new version renders in about 25 seconds while viewers keep the old one. Padding, corner radius and a stacked portrait layout are in the design but not in the painter yet, so 9:16 shows the current layouts as they are.
