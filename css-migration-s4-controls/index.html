S4: editor controls and property panel, before and after

Before is the editor frame PR head (#721). After is S4: 06-forms.css, 08-color-picker.css, 09-dynamic-lists.css, 11-form-editor.css and 18-property-panel.css are deleted. The property panel for every block kind, the Style pop-down and the colour picker are built from shared controls: EditorField, ControlRow, TextField, TitleField, ColorField, a DynamicList for FAQ questions, pricing plans, features, form fields and logos, plus a SegmentedControl and Slider primitive. Five text-input looks, three segmented controls and several remove-button styles are now one of each.

Parity came first: against a base captured twice, the per-state style capture showed zero of 943,420 values changed; the only real change was the editor preview's pricing cards, which a deleted rule had also styled and which now lay out like the published page. The editor used local fixture pages, so nothing was saved or published. The differences below are the normalize wave and include decisions for review: small Buttons are 12px (were 12.8px), panel fields are 14px with the primitive's focus ring, labels are 12px, FAQ questions sit on cards like pricing plans, every remove button is the same quiet X, and a field at its length limit shows only a red count. Fixed along the way: the lead form overflowing the panel, the logo remove button unreachable by keyboard, the account name field showing no focus, and the colour picker resetting its hue to 0 at the maximum. With keyboard focus forced, every control now shows a focus indicator; 155 showed none before.
