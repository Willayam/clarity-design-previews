5 · Decision required before consolidation

Choose one supported backend direction

convex-spike/ is the current AI generation service. It is production code despite its name. The app also retains Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, Durable Objects and a media container, with WorkOS, Stripe and Resend as deliberate service dependencies.

Two open directions overlap: consolidate five Workers into app/media/producer, or move email, workspace data and orchestration to Convex, with Modal proposed for media processing. Completing both without a sequence would create throwaway migration work.

Competing consolidation plans→One ownership/deployment decision→Delete redundant service seams

Recommendation: finish the current data migrations first; retain Convex for its supported AI role and give its package a production name. Decide broader Convex adoption separately from Phoenix retirement. Consolidate app services only where their deployment/auth seam has no useful independence. Keep media serving and container lifecycle isolation where they earn their keep.

Benefit: deep modules retain testable interfaces while removing transport and deploy complexity. “All TypeScript” or “all Cloudflare” would be an additional platform objective; replacing working media processing solely for language uniformity does not complete a legacy migration.
