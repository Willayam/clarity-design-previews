Claims rejected during review

  • Duplicate migration-number prefixes alone do not require renumbering. Installed Wrangler compares full filenames against applied names. Rewriting applied migrations needs its own justified plan.
  • No evidence establishes that the agent-docs PR666 must merge before GoHighLevel PR645.
  • An older draft such as PR631 is not automatically obsolete.
  • PR680's /file/training_videos fallback is different from /api/folder/public_training_video. Their similar names do not create a dependency.
  • Zero repository callers does not establish zero old-client traffic.

Principles that changed the choice

Separate Before Serializing Shared State led to a read-only compatibility audit instead of another branch editing the same account model. Subtract Before You Add led to completing legacy deletion contracts before another backend migration. Outcome-Oriented Execution means the account cutover must include the rules introduced by incoming PRs, rather than preserve a permanent bypass.
