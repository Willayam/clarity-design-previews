S1: surface languages into primitives, before and after

Before is the journey-namespace PR head (d66248e9). After is S1 (49b70b07): button-surface.css and chrome.css are deleted. Button faces live in button.tsx, a Surface component provides hero | panel | bar | media through React context that Button reads, the chrome parts (segmented control, chip, separator) own their classes, and only two @utility blocks remain in index.css: glass and glass-media.

Parity came first: the button gallery (every button on eight routes in rest, hover, focus, expanded, pressed, disabled and invalid states, 368,768 computed values) matched the base exactly, and all 37 screenshots matched. The differences below are the normalize wave's intended changes: the dashed "add" labels in panels snap from 12.8px to 12px, the split-button seam insets 8px instead of 7.2px, panel buttons stay flat on hover, panel controls show their focus and invalid rings again, and primary and outline buttons turn their border red when invalid. Screenshots at 1440×1800, thumbnails masked.
