Trim flow prototypes

Throwaway prototypes. Use the bottom bar or the ← and → keys to switch variants.

1. Trim precision

  • 0 Current. Today's trimmer, for comparison.
  • A Fine scrub. Drag away from the strip to slow down.
  • B Zoom on hold. Hold a handle still and the strip zooms to 6 s.
  • C Overview plus detail. A thin overview plus a 10 s detail strip.
  • D Mark and nudge. I/O at the playhead, frame nudges, typed times.
  • E Transcript trim. Click a word to start or end there.
  • F Auto trim to speech. One click cuts the dead air; handles snap to speech.

2. Trim processing and journey page editing

  • A Stepper in the modal. Named stages, plus what viewers see right now.
  • B Optimistic preview. Your player plays the cut at once; a pill tracks the render.
  • C Ambient. The modal closes, and a toast, a tray and badges carry progress.
  • D Two versions. The full video and the new cut are shown as rows that swap.

Every processing variant also splits the journey page video's actions into Edit video and Replace. Use the bottom bar to step through the lifecycle, including the retry and failure paths.
