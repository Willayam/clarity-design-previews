P0b: cascade environment, before and after

Before is the normalized head of PR #695 (31ccd5fa). After is P0b (df45997a): legacy partials moved under Tailwind's utilities layer, 00-base.css retired, app chrome kept on the app palette inside the preview. Every difference below is the primitives' type applying for the first time on authenticated routes, because button, input, textarea, select { font: inherit } no longer outranks their utilities. Captured by tests/visual/home-library.spec.ts at 1440×1800 with thumbnails masked.

The structural probe (25 routes × 6 widths, 11.6 million values, deterministic) grouped the change into 25 cause rows, all type: font-size, line-height, and font-weight on buttons, inputs, selects, and tabs. Layering every sheet would have produced 217 rows including a full app repaint from a raw sidebar colour, so two sheets stay unlayered until their own slices.
