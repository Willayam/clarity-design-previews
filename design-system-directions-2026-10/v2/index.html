V2 Refined current

The current Clarity with its depth budget cut to four edge treatments and one translucent level. The structure stays: sidebar and content, 16:9 media cards, the page editor with its right-hand properties panel, the dark bar over video. What goes is the stacking: bevels on buttons, outlines on everything, hover states that add a second fill on top of the first.

Live responds to your pointer and keyboard. Simulated is a class that paints the state so it can be compared side by side. Everything on this sheet is sample data.

Depth: one treatment per surface

Every surface picks exactly one. Tone for panels on canvas, a hairline where tone cannot separate (sidebar, lists, form fields), shadow for raised things in light mode, glass for the one floating level. The fifth tile is the pattern this direction removes.

ToneCards' thumbnails, panels, folder tiles, dropzones. Surface on canvas, no border, no shadow.
HairlineSidebar edge, list rows, form fields, accordion rows, dark-mode raised surfaces. 1px at 10% alpha, 18% on fields.
ShadowMenus, dialogs, toasts, the paper inside a document card. Light mode only; dark mode uses a hairline.
GlassPlayer bar, recorder dock, section toolbar, selection bar, chips and buttons over media. Tint, blur, one edge.
StackedGradient fill, outline, inner highlight, inner shade and drop shadow on one control. Removed everywhere.

Space, radius, size

A 4px grid. Five radii plus the pill; small buttons and fields share the 8px of their full-size versions. Three control heights with a fine pointer. On a coarse pointer, and at 720px and under, the two smaller ones become 44 and the large one 48; segment buttons are 40 inside their 44 track. Labels and glyphs keep their size.

Flat fills, one primary per view. Hover changes the fill and nothing else. Busy keeps the label and adds a spinner. The rest column is live; hover, pressed and focus are simulated so they can be compared in one glance.

Fields and selection

Form fields carry one hairline and gain the accent on focus. Errors turn the hairline red and add a line of text under the field. The toolbar search is tonal because it sits among tonal controls. No inset shadows, no inner highlights.

Media card

The thumbnail is the surface. There is no container, no border and no lift on hover. The first card carries the long title from the Library to show the two-line clamp.

  • AlwaysDuration chip, bottom right, tabular, glass-media tint. Every chip over a thumbnail uses this one recipe.
  • AlwaysTitle (two lines, then an ellipsis) and one meta line with the kind glyph, below the image, not over it. Each segment is its own span, so narrow phones drop the trailing ones instead of clipping a word.
  • When neededStatus or type chip bottom left: Draft, Live, PDF. One at a time. Processing covers the image instead.
  • On hover or focusPlay glyph centred and More top right; Share is in its menu. On a coarse pointer, and at 720px and under, More is always visible at 44px and nothing else sits on the image.
  • While selectingSelect box top left, once More, then Select has started a selection on any card, or when the box has keyboard focus.
  • SelectedA 2px accent ring around the thumbnail, nothing else changes.
  • RemovedCard container, outline, frosted stage behind every item, title overlay on the image, gradient scrims, hover lift, second hover fill, badges for views or dates, inline action rows.
  • RemovedPortrait video letterboxed on a gradient. It gets a 9:16 frame on a blurred copy of its own colours.
