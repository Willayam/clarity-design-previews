What support gets

  • /admin/users searches on the server, shows what each account's access rests on and until when, filters by organization, access and "ends within 30 days", and exports CSV. The Boardroom organization filter lists every member: that is the Boardroom report.
  • /admin/users/:id is one panel: a headline sentence (can this person get in, why, until when, who changed it last), every grant including ended ones, Stripe subscriptions, organizations, staff, suspension, history and content counts.
  • Actions, each one audited command: Grant access (trial, complimentary, included), Change end, End now, Suspend (typed email) and Unsuspend, Make staff and Remove staff, plus Billing links, Change email and Access account. Refusals come back as a sentence, for example "You cannot suspend your own account."
  • Boardroom members sign up at /boardroom and get 10 months of included access paid by the Boardroom organization, tagged by membership.
