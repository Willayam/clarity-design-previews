S7: recorder and video edit modal, before and after

Before is the video stats PR head (#720). After is S7: 19-record-video.css and 22-video-edit-modal.css are deleted together (the audit on #704 showed that layering either alone breaks the other's states). /record (device menus, layout tiles, record action, finished take, trimmer, filmstrip, preview picker) and the video edit modal (summary timeline, trim dock, preview selection, save states) are styled in their components on the primitives.

Parity came first: against a base captured twice, the structural probe showed zero differing values over 228 captures and screenshots matched apart from 1 to 50 pixels of antialiasing at the glass dock's corners, which the base shows against itself. Camera and screen are fake media streams; every upload, save and render request is answered locally. Breakpoints behave as before at ±1px: the 60rem container query with the sidebar open and collapsed, and the 640, 760, 768 and 780px queries. The differences below are the normalize wave and include decisions for review: the recorder header uses the editor's compact header (title 22px bold becomes 16px medium); the video dock's controls use the video player's see-through bar face, so the bright blue play circle becomes a quiet icon; Still and Moving Preview are toggle buttons instead of tabs; the finished take is a single column (on main a two-column grid squeezed the video to about 160px between 761 and 1100px with the sidebar open); the microphone level meter sits inside its dropdown button.
