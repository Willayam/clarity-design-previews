Findings worth your attention

  • Playback "failures" were mostly crawlers. Diagnosis: 13 of 22 failures came from datacenter networks, mostly Microsoft's. Real viewers fail 4.25%, under Mux's 4.9% average. The "70% never play" number counted hidden players loading without a tap. Tickets WIL-3501 to WIL-3504.
  • Video start is still slow. Mux median startup went from 693 ms to 1,580 ms after preload="none". Warming the first player brought back 1.8 to 4.6 MB of video before a tap, so it was dropped. This needs a product call on how many bytes to spend for a faster start.
  • The portrait fix reaches few videos. Only 36 of 7,516 production videos carry mux_aspect_ratio. Older pages need a backfill, which fits WIL-3494's lifecycle work.
  • The email flip is undone. clarity-a0 merged #908 (Cloudflare email, behavior unchanged) and ended before its one-word production EMAIL_DELIVERY flip. That flip is yours.
