## Your direction: v04 "Bottom dock" (file `variants/v04-bottom-dock.html`, model gpt-6-astra)

A persistent composer docked at the bottom centre of the canvas, the width of the page column. Precedent: Notion AI's bottom bar, Linear Ask, Perplexity. The Write page header button goes away; the dock replaces it. The thread grows upward from the dock as a solid sheet over the lower part of the canvas and collapses back to the composer plus one live status line, so the page stays readable while work runs. Tool steps are Marker rows inside the thread; changes are inline chips that jump to the block. The dock never covers the block that changed: when the rep clicks a change, the canvas scrolls it above the dock. On a phone the dock sits at the bottom like a messaging app and the thread opens full screen. On Home the same dock sits at the bottom of the dashboard.
