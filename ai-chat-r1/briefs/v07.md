## Your direction: v07 "Command bar" (file `variants/v07-command-bar.html`, model gpt-6-astra)

Keyboard first. Cmd+K, or the header button, opens a centred command bar over the dimmed page. The rep types a request; the answer and its tool steps stream directly beneath the bar, which then holds a compact thread for quick follow-ups. Esc tucks the thread into a small pill at the bottom right of the canvas that shows the last result and Undo, and reopens the bar on click. Precedent: Raycast AI, Arc's command bar, Superhuman. The bet: most requests are one-shot with a quick follow-up, and the page should come back to full view the moment the work is done. On a phone the bar is a full-screen sheet with the composer at the bottom. On Home the same bar opens over the dashboard.
