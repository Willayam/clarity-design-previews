## Your direction: v05 "At the block" (file `variants/v05-at-the-block.html`, model gpt-6-astra)

Direct manipulation. The chat is anchored to what the rep is looking at. Hovering or selecting a page block shows a small Ask affordance on it, and the composer carries a scope chip ("Whole page", or "Title" when the title is selected) so "make this punchier" needs no block name. The thread lives in a narrow right rail that can collapse to just numbered pins on the canvas. Changes appear on the canvas as numbered pins on the changed blocks; the title shows its before and after inline at the block, with Undo there too, like a suggestion in a document. Precedent: Figma comments, Notion's inline AI, Google Docs suggestions. On a phone, tapping a block opens a bottom sheet scoped to it, and a header button opens the full thread. On Home, the scope chip becomes the selected page card(s) and the rail shows the stats answer.
