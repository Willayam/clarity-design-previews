## Your direction: v06 "One assistant everywhere" (file `variants/v06-one-assistant.html`, model fable)

An app-level assistant. "Ask Clarity" is a first-class entry in the app sidebar (above Home and Leads) and on Cmd+J, opening a resizable right panel in the app shell. One conversation follows the rep across Home, Library and the editor, so a thread started on Home continues when they open the page it made. The composer carries a context chip for where the rep is ("Acme follow-up, draft"), removable. Answers render as objects the rep can act on in place: a page card with Open and Share, a video card, a leads list whose rows offer "Draft a follow-up". Precedent: Shopify Sidekick, Linear Ask, Slack AI. In the editor the header's Write page button becomes the same entry. On a phone the assistant is a full-screen view reached from the mobile nav.
