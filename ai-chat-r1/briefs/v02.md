## Your direction: v02 "Chat and preview" (file `variants/v02-chat-and-preview.html`, model opus)

Chat-first split. When the chat is open, the editor becomes two columns: the conversation on the left (about 420 px, right of the icon rail) and the page on the right as the conversation's output, with a thin toolbar over it (Draft status, desktop or phone preview toggle, a way back to the full editor). Precedent: v0, Lovable, Claude's artifacts. The bet: making a page is a conversation, and the page is what it produces. Each turn that changed the page ends in a compact "draft revision" card that focuses the preview on what changed; small numbered markers on the preview point back to the turn. On a phone, a two-tab switch (Chat, Page) at the top. On Home, the left column is the same chat and the right column shows the answer as a result (the stats as a proper table the rep can act on).
