Decisions for William

  1. Swedish speed. Whisper on Workers AI sets the floor. In this run Swedish medians were 2.0 to 2.8 s after a pause and 3.4 to 5.5 s while talking. English was 1.6 to 1.9 s and 2.6 to 4.1 s in the same hour, and 0.2 s and 1.2 to 2.9 s on a calmer day. Groq runs the same Whisper model in 0.2 to 0.35 s per chunk, which would put Swedish level with English. The audio would then leave Cloudflare, and every environment would need a Groq key.
  2. Mux words for Swedish. The brief said nothing may read Mux captions for a non-English take, and this PR follows it. But on 7 of 7 Swedish takes, Mux Robots returned correct Swedish words, labelled sv. Relaxing the rule to the English subtitle track only would give Swedish uploads titles and give a Swedish take a backup when live speech fails.
  3. Captions. Mux still adds generated English subtitles to every recording, so a Swedish take's player can show English captions invented from Swedish speech. That needs its own change.
