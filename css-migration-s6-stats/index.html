S6: video stats modal, before and after

Before is the video stats modal as production renders it today, with 25-video-stats-modal.css unlayered (captured with main's cascade). After is S6: the stylesheet is deleted and the modal is styled in VideoStatsModal.tsx on the Dialog primitive, with ButtonBase sort headers, DialogDescription for the summary, and the table columns as a theme token.

Parity came first: at the parity commit the structural probe (40 captures at 1440, 1024, 721, 720, 719 and 390) showed zero differences and all 64 screenshots matched their base. Stats data is stubbed with generated views on fixture videos. The differences below are the normalize wave: the modal keeps its 928px width but takes the Dialog primitive's padding, gap and radius; below 720px the padding is 24px instead of 16px, so the table is 16px narrower on a phone; hover styles apply only on devices that can hover.
