Live training banner: round 2

Seven louder versions built from Dark spotlight, Brand feature and Calendar tile. Every one leads with a benefit, shows how soon the session is, and has one clear action, Add to calendar. That opens the landing page, where the calendar buttons are.

Every version has the ✕ in its top corner. Dismissing hides the banner until the next session, two weeks later. Each one runs in the real app on Home, captured 20 October 2026 in Pacific time. Round 1 is here.

A. Dark calendar Dark spotlight plus Calendar tile

A dark navy card with a glow, a large calendar date, a countdown pill and a benefit headline. Add to calendar is the one solid button. It combines all three of your favorites.

B. Dark host Dark spotlight plus Todd

Todd's face with a pulsing ring and a LIVE tag, and the headline Join Todd Hartley live. People respond to a person. The photo is a frame from his Learn video, so a real headshot would look sharper.

C. Bold blue Brand feature, turned up

A solid Clarity-blue card with white text and the boldest promise, Close more deals with Clarity. It stands out most on the light Home page.

D. Countdown Dark spotlight plus a live clock

Days, hours and minutes count down to the session and tick while the page is open. Urgency does the selling. When the session starts, the clock reaches zero.

E. Brand tile with checks Brand feature plus Calendar tile

The light blue gradient from Brand feature with an inverted calendar tile and three checkmarks: the time, Free for every Clarity user, and Tips you can use right away. It's the lightest of the seven and still clearly louder than today's banner.

F. Poster Dark spotlight plus a video frame

Half of the card is Todd on camera with a LIVE badge, like an event poster. It stands out strongly, and on a phone the photo takes about half the banner.

G. Topic spotlight Dark spotlight plus the session's topic

The headline is the session's real topic, Mastering Your Clarity Journey Page Prompts, with Add to calendar and What you'll learn. A specific topic sells best. It needs one line of code per session with the topic. Without that line, it would fall back to the general title.
