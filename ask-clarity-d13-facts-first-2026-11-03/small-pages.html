Give Lumen Health’s 40-person Customer Success team a clearer way to manage onboarding as it takes on about 60 new customers each quarter.
A clearer path from kickoff to customer value
hero
Lumen Health’s Customer Success team brings on about 60 new customers each quarter. With 40 people supporting that work, every kickoff needs a clear path, shared ownership and dependable follow-through. Today, onboarding work lives across Asana and a shared Google Sheet, while kickoff takes four weeks on average.

For Priya Shah, Head of Customer Success, the opportunity is to make the process easier to see and coordinate as the team grows. A forwardable plan gives her and VP Daniel Okafor a shared starting point: what the team is managing today, where the handoffs can get difficult, and what a better-supported kickoff could look like.
Onboarding at Lumen Health today
story
When the work is split between Asana and a shared Google Sheet, teams may need to move between places to understand progress, ownership and next steps. At a volume of about 60 new customers per quarter, even small gaps in visibility can make coordination harder across a 40-person Customer Success team.

A four-week average kickoff is a useful baseline—not a verdict on the team. The practical question is whether the process gives everyone the information and clear handoffs they need to keep each customer moving. Making that workflow easier to follow can help the team manage a steady stream of new customers without losing sight of individual kickoffs.
Keep handoffs visible as volume grows
story
The team currently relies on familiar tools: Asana and a shared Google Sheet. Those tools are part of the current workflow, and any proposed change should be clear about how it fits alongside them. There is no Asana integration today; an integration is being looked at, but it should not be treated as available.

That makes a grounded evaluation important. Rather than assume a connection that does not exist, Priya and Daniel can assess the workflow on its actual capabilities and decide what would need to change—or remain in place—for the team to use it. The goal is a well-informed decision, with the current process and any limitations stated plainly.
Evaluate the workflow as it exists today
story
A useful next step is to review how the proposed approach would support Lumen Health’s customer kickoff process: the work the team tracks, the handoffs it manages and the visibility it needs across 40 people. That discussion should begin with the current reality—about 60 new customers per quarter, a four-week average kickoff, and work split between Asana and a shared Google Sheet.

Security is part of the decision, too. Lumen Health’s CTO will want to review the security material before anything is signed, so that review belongs in the evaluation process. With the expected decision by the end of the quarter, Priya can forward this page to Daniel as a concise basis for aligning on workflow fit, security review and the next decision steps.

g14-request-boundary, step 2

Give Globex employees one secure, familiar way to access the tools they need. Single sign-on lets people sign in through their organization’s identity provider instead of managing a separate password for each application.
Discuss SSO
A simpler, more secure sign-in for Globex
hero
Every separate application login adds another password for employees to remember and another access point for administrators to manage. As teams grow, keeping access consistent across tools can become harder—especially when people join, change roles, or leave. Single sign-on (SSO) connects application access to an organization’s identity provider, so employees can use the sign-in process they already know.

For Globex, SSO can help reduce login friction while giving administrators a more centralized way to manage access. Employees spend less time juggling credentials, and access decisions can follow the identity and policies managed by the organization. The result is a clearer path into the tools people need, without asking every user to maintain another standalone password. Explore how SSO works and consider how it could fit Globex’s existing sign-in and access practices.
