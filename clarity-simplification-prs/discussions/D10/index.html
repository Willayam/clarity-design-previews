Clarity simplification · discussion D10

Blocks: keep pricing and summary apart; phone optional; consent wording

Pricing and summary blocks, the lead form defaults and the consent line on the public page. The consent text is a compliance call.

Recommendation

Do not fold summary into pricing. Seven recent pages use both and two use only a summary; a merged block makes the pricing editor carry a table for those nine, and 1,697 legacy pricing blocks would need the new shape. U17 renames the tile to "Summary table" and U19 drops the three-tier chip tonight, which is the real complaint.

New forms start with phone optional (existing forms keep their flags). The consent line becomes "I agree to be contacted by email or text about this proposal." It keeps the text-message consent the current line carries, in words a prospect reads. Whoever chose the current wording should confirm it is still enough for texting.

The problem

Pricing is on 7% of recent customer pages and summary on 3%, and the audit asked whether they should be one block. The form defaults make phone required and show a consent line written for a billing system ("transactional messages related to my account, orders, or services I have requested") on a sales page.

What it would look like

Before, the form editor and the public form today. After, phone optional and the new consent line.

Options

Keep both blocks, phone optional, plain consent (recommended)

Three small copy and default changes, no migration.

Fold summary into pricing

A pricing editor with an optional table, a migration for 9 summary blocks and a new shape for 1,697 pricing blocks, for two pages that use a summary without pricing.

Keep phone required

Reps get a phone on every lead. Prospects who will not give a phone leave nothing. No local data on which matters more.

Questions for William

1. Keep pricing and summary as two blocks?

Recommended: Yes.

2. Phone optional for new forms (existing forms keep required)?

Recommended: Yes. The email is what the view notification and the lead row need; a phone can be asked for on the call.

3. Compliance: is "I agree to be contacted by email or text about this proposal." enough for the texting the GoHighLevel sync enables, or must the transactional-messages wording stay?

Recommended: Use the new line unless the person who wrote the current one says texting rules need the old phrasing. That is a check, not a design choice.
