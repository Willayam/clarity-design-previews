Stageshow audit of Clarity's main workflows

2 November 2026. Staging at 13fa9c5f3, PR 1610 preview at 86f09cf64, recorder designs round 3. Test accounts and test footage only.

What this is. Stageshow builds faux product demos. It has no audit mode, and its film script opens a logged-out browser and needs a demo stage. So I loaded its check engine into a signed-in real browser, drove the real flows with a fake camera, and judged the frames against its craft and motion rules. Its demo-only rules, such as hero crops, filler bars and loops, do not apply to a real app, so I ignored them.

The verdict. The strongest moment is Stop. The finish screen keeps the picture exactly in place and the title lands in under 2 seconds, which is the flow Stageshow describes as one continuous object. The weakest stretches are the take itself, which still looks like setup, and the hand-off at Build page. I would pick v12 from Round 3, because it more than doubles the picture during a take, and fix its Stop button and its 96 px pop-out first. The independent reviewer preferred v13, for a reason worth taking seriously, so that call is yours. The review section has both sides.

Fix these first

  1. Start and Stop fall out of the window on short laptops, and PR 1610 makes it worse.
  2. Home calls a finished page Untitled Video, under a thumbnail showing its real title.
  3. Apply turns the picture black in PR 1610 for the whole render.
  4. v12's Stop is narrower than its Pause, and its 96 px pop-out puts a mis-click 6 px from ending the take.
  5. Three of four layout tiles do nothing in a browser with no screen share.

18

In v12, Stop is narrower than Pause

Fix firstRound 3 design

Stop measures 83 px and Pause 116 px. Start was 346 px wide a second earlier. Stop is the action a rep most needs to find without looking, and it is the smallest thing in the bar. v11 gives Stop 224 px and v13 242 px.

Fix. Use the design's own drawer Stop on the bar. It is 222 px wide, shows the time, and sits exactly where Start was. That keeps Stop under the rep's hand, which was the reviewer's objection to v12. Also swap the round record dot on Stop for the square production uses.

Rule: one red record action, heaviest mark where the story is (craft.md).

19

The 360x96 pop-out cannot carry five tiles

Fix firstRound 3 design

The case for v12 on the board was that the same row of tiles works in the pop-out. At 360x260 it does. At 360x96 the tiles are 32 px wide with no labels, so Bubble, Cut-out and Camera are hard to tell apart, and the last tile is 6 px from a 71 px Stop. One slip while talking to a prospect in another window ends the take. v11 has an 8 px gap and v13 has 6 px, so all three share this.

Fix. At 96 px tall, drop the tiles and keep the timer, Stop and Pause. Or show one tile for the current style, with a menu.

Rule: "Can you read the important labels?" (teaching.md, Desktop at phone width).

20

After recording, Apply is live with nothing to apply

Fix soonRound 3 design

With "As recorded" selected, Apply is enabled in all three designs (disabled false, opacity 1). In v12 it also shows solid blue while Build page is solid blue, so the screen has two primary actions, and it is not clear which one a rep should press next.

Fix. Disable Apply until a change exists, and keep one blue action on screen at a time.

Rule: your own lab rule on controls that do not change the picture.

22

Empty space at setup

Fix soonRound 3 design

v12 leaves about 250 px empty under the device row, and a gap of about 240 px inside the drawer above Start. The picture is limited by width at 1440x900, so it cannot grow into the space. Moving the device pickers into the drawer fills one gap and lifts the other. v11 and v13 share this.

Recording today

1

Start and Stop fall out of the window on short laptops

Fix firstToday, mainPR 1610

With a screen layout on, Start ends at 678 px in a 660 px window at 1366x660. At 1280x600 it is off screen in every layout. PR 1610 adds a 48 px row, which cuts Start off at 1440x790 too, at 797 against 790. All numbers are measured in the running app.

1440x790 is my estimate of Chrome's inner window on a 13-inch MacBook Air. Stop sits in the same row as Start in every frame I filmed, so a rep mid-take would have to scroll to stop. That last part is inferred, since I measured Start and did not film a take at these sizes.

Fix. Size the preview from the window height as well as the width. Every Round 3 design already keeps Start and Stop on screen at all three sizes, so porting one also fixes this.

Rule: cut controls (craft.md). "Is anything cut that the eye needs whole?" (verify.md).

2

Home calls a finished page Untitled Video

Fix firstToday, main

After the AI built the page, its title and the editor top bar both read "Turn a one-minute video into the next meeting", and they still did after a reload. Home lists the same page as Untitled Video, on desktop and phone. The card's own thumbnail shows the real title right above the wrong name.

I saw this on the one built page I checked. I did not trace the cause. The video itself does get its title, so this looks specific to the page's library name.

Fix. When the page's library name is still the default, write the title to it, as the take title already does for the video.

Rule: the payoff is visible and stays on screen (motion.md, Story). A rep's Home is the record of what they have sent.

4

Three of the four layout tiles do nothing in a browser with no screen share

Fix firstToday, mainRound 3 design

Corrected 2026-11-03. I first wrote that phones have no screen share. That is wrong. iPhone Safari 27 has getDisplayMedia and these tiles work there. My test removed the API, so this finding only describes browsers without it, such as older iPhones. There, pressing 1/3 split leaves Camera selected and nothing changes, and the same holds for the other screen tiles, which look just as available. The tiles also have no labels at this width. v12 at 390 keeps Split and Screen tiles too.

Fix. Hide the screen tiles only when the browser has no getDisplayMedia, never by device. With one layout left, drop the row and give the height to the picture.

Rule: your own lab rule, "A control that does not change the picture must not look available".

5

"Record screen only" is a dead button in a browser with no screen share

Fix soonToday, main

Corrected 2026-11-03. This applies to browsers with no getDisplayMedia, not to phones in general. iPhone Safari 27 has it. I reproduced this at 390 wide with no getDisplayMedia and a camera that returns NotFoundError or OverconstrainedError. The app says "You can still record your screen" and offers the button. Pressing it changes nothing. NotReadableError shows Reload instead, so the trigger is fairly rare. Start recording also stays enabled here. The copy is at RecorderStatusZone.tsx:35.

Fix. Show the button only when the browser can share a screen. Otherwise offer Reload or the camera picker.

Rule: a control reads true. A rep with a broken camera has nothing else to try on this screen.

6

A dark line and corner ticks on the video frame, on the page prospects see

Fix soonToday, main

On light footage, the public page shows a near-black line about 2 px tall along the top of the video, and gray arcs at the corners. The recorder preview, finish screen and editor show the arcs. Dark footage hides both, which is probably why this has survived. A screen recording of a white slide deck is common for reps (my guess at their usage).

I did not trace the cause. My guess is a dark backing behind the player showing through at the rounded corners, plus a rounding gap between the poster and the frame.

Fix. Remove the dark backing once the poster has painted, or match it to the poster. Check at 1x on a white slide.

Rule: "Zoom into edges and corners: rounded corners intact" (rehearse.md).

7

Arriving at the editor: black frame, covered title

Fix soonToday, main

After Build page, the video block is solid black for about 1.4 s (three frames of a coarse sheet), though the same poster was on screen a moment earlier. The composer then opens over the page title and cuts it at its edge. On a phone it covers the top half of the screen. The composer's own button is also called Build page, so the rep presses the same words twice.

Fix. Carry the poster across the navigation. Open the composer where it covers nothing the rep is reading, such as below the video. Give the second button a label that says what it does next.

Rule: "No holes or blank frames" (motion.md). "Space follows the story" (craft.md).

8

The trim handles snap 0.7 s after Stop

Fix soonToday, main

Stageshow's jump scan caught this one. In this take the Auto trim landed in one frame, with the left handle jumping 68 px and the range shrinking 95 px, and the selector flipped from None to Auto around the same moment. This happens 0.7 s after Stop, while the rep is looking right at the strip. The scan also caught the action row drawing 116 px too high for a frame or two at Stop.

Fix. Ease the handles from full range to the Auto range over about 250 ms, so the rep sees what the trim did. Reserve the editor's height before it mounts.

Rule: "Every change moves" and "After motion nothing moves" (motion.md).

13

Independent review

Stageshow's last step is a fresh-eye review, by someone who has not seen the builder's reasons. I gave gpt-6-astra the 44 frames and Stageshow's review brief, and none of my findings. It did not change any file.

Where it agreed, unprompted. The black picture after Apply (finding 15). The composer covering the title (7). The black player on arrival (7). The second player in the Style tab (16). It also liked the same things, the share dialog and the re-record dialog.

What it added, and I kept. The paused state (11), the unlabeled trim group (12), the missing timer on v12's phone take (23), and the round dot on Stop (18). I checked each against its frame.

Where it disagreed: v13 over v12. Its argument is that v13 keeps the picture and Stop exactly where they were at setup, while v12 resizes the picture and moves the controls at the moment a rep starts talking. It also faults v13 for its mostly empty, full-height drawer. Both points hold up against the frames. Your own lab rule, "Nothing shifts", argues for v13. My case for v12 is that the picture is twice the size for the whole take, and the shift happens once, on a press, before the rep starts talking. Using v12's own 222 px Stop in Start's exact spot removes most of the objection. Neither of us has seen a real rep use either one, and a short take on each with a real camera would settle it better than this argument can.

What I set aside, and why. It flagged the generated page for telling the reader to send sales videos instead of addressing Dana at Northwind. That comes from my test fixture, whose speech is about sales videos, so the page summarized what it heard. It did mention Northwind. It also flagged that Copy link shows no lasting confirmation. The button does show "Copied" for about 2 s, and its still was simply taken after that. I checked that in the video.

How much to trust this

What ran. 156 stills and 93 filmed transitions across sign-in, Home, recorder, finish screen, Build page, share, public page, settings, PR 1610 and the three designs, at 1440x900 and 390x844, with short windows at 1440x790, 1366x660 and 1280x600. The engine printed 1,743 still lines, 125 layout lines, 236 motion lines and 197 jump lines.

Most of those lines are noise on a real app. Many are one fault repeated across frames. The rest fire on things that are fine here: loose list on control rows, uneven gutters on page layout, the mic meter's hard edge, touching on screen-reader text, and clipped on Home's miniature page renders. near-empty frames on the Videos tab only meant fewer cards. I treated a line as a finding only after seeing it in a frame.

Who found what. Stageshow's checks found the trim snap, the 116 px flash and the phone's 0 px gap. Findings 1, 9, 13, 18, 19 and 20 are measurements from my own scripts. The rest came from reading frames against Stageshow's rules. So the rules did more for this audit than the checker did.

Sampling. 55 of the 93 filmed windows had complete frame coverage. 38 had gaps and count as inconclusive for motion, mostly on heavy pages, where the checks themselves slow the page. An absence of motion lines in those windows proves nothing.

Not checked. A real camera, a real screen share, a real phone, Safari or iOS, a live Document Picture in Picture window (only the designs' mock of it), keyboard focus, touch, reduced motion, and the look of a finished restyle. I also did not put the frames beside Stageshow's gold examples. Those are marketing scenes, and a comparison would not be fair to a working product.

Rig caveats. Audio capture on the test Mac stopped answering partway through the audit. The phone run used the repo's speech fixture through WebAudio instead, as record-take does, which is why phone frames say "Choose microphone". The designs are mocks, so their black side bars come from the mock screen image.
