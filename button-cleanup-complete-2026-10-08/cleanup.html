# Button cleanup ledger

The approved consolidation and the remaining mechanical button cleanup are implemented locally. This records the completed scope and the remaining intentional geometry. It does not claim signed-in or production parity.

## Completed

- All seven approved patterns use shared defaults or approved variants.
- Contact add and remove controls use xs and icon sizes instead of recreating their dimensions.
- Billing card removal uses destructive-quiet, has an accessible name, and exposes pending work.
- Six raw app button sites now use ButtonBase. Upload cards use a noninteractive shell around their own actions.
- Four button-styled navigation sites use native anchors or router Links and buttonVariants. Disabled SSO has no destination and cannot start a handoff.
- Icon controls have explicit names. Editor media alignment, sizing and removal work by keyboard and fire once for a mouse press.
- Floating media controls reveal on focus. Alt+F10 reaches floating text controls; moving focus between them keeps the toolbar mounted.
- Existing lint and CSS allowances only shrink. Raw app buttons and caller paint remain guarded.

## What stays

The existing hero, panel, bar and media faces stay in the shared primitive. Legacy destructive callers keep their reviewed context. Customer-branded Journey Page forms keep their reset and submit buttons so LivePreview matches the published renderer. ButtonBase owns no action face; editor overlay geometry stays with its feature. Its glass variant reuses the established shared material.
