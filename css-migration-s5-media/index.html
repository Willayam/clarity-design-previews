S5: media surfaces, before and after

Before is the surfaces PR head (#715). After is S5: MediaPicker.css, MediaPickerDialog.css, VideoPlayer.css, JourneyMediaResizer.css, and the library and learn half of 14-library.css are deleted. The side-panel picker, the canvas media overlay, the picker dialog, the video player and the resize handle are styled in their components on the Surface and Button primitives.

Parity came first: at the parity checkpoint, the structural probe (34 scenes, 80 captures including the 719/720/721, 759/760/761 and 519/520/521 breakpoint boundaries and a held resize) showed zero differences, the button gallery zero of 75,078 values, and all 143 screenshots matched. The differences below are the normalize wave: spacing, type and the player controls bar snap to the scale, the controls grid becomes flex, and the picker dialog takes the Dialog primitive's overlay, radius, border and shadow (8px wider at 720px and below). Captures at 1440×1800 plus narrow widths, card thumbnails masked; media inside the picker overlay and player is not masked.
