iPhone capture probe

This tests what Clarity's multi-track recorder can rely on. It records camera and screen as two separate files, on this phone only. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Tap Camera + mic and allow both.
  2. Tap Share screen and start sharing.
  3. Tap Record. Talk for about 5 seconds.
  4. Swipe to the home screen. Open Notes or Settings and scroll for about 10 seconds. Keep talking.
  5. Come back to Safari. Wait 5 seconds.
  6. Open another Safari tab for about 5 seconds, then come back.
  7. Turn the phone sideways for 5 seconds, then back upright.
  8. Tap Stop. Watch both clips, then tap Copy results and paste them to Claude.
