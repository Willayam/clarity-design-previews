iPhone capture probe v2.1

Third round, same page. Sync check now unlocks only after you have left Safari and come back, because that step is the one still unmeasured. This page also measures sync between the camera and screen files, whether your voice survives while you are in another app, and what rotation does to each file. Everything stays on this phone.

  1. Tap Camera + mic, then Share screen.
  2. Tap Record. A dark screen appears and flashes white three times. Look at the phone and keep talking.
  3. Go to another app for about 10 seconds. Keep talking, say what you see.
  4. Come back and tap Sync check. It flashes three more times.
  5. Turn the phone sideways for about 5 seconds, then upright again.
  6. Tap Stop. Analysis takes up to a minute. Then tap Copy results and paste them to Claude.

Indoors helps: the flashes light your face, and the camera has to see that.
