What every card shares

  • The link row has the URL, underlined and opening in a new tab, and one copy icon. Off or never published, the row dims and the copy icon disables.
  • The band is bottom left beside the primary. Green Live when up to date, amber Live with the freshness line when edits are pending, grey Not published or Off otherwise.
  • Choosing "No one" or "Off" asks first. The confirm says the link stops working for everyone you sent it to, and that Publish turns the same link back on.
  • Choosing "Anyone with the link" while off or never published does the same as the Publish button.
  • Password protected is its own switch row with a lock, and the password input under it when on. C7 is the one exception and is labelled.
